About

Hi, I'm Alvaro. I make web products that hold up once real customers start using them.

Most of my work sits where a website stops being a brochure and starts doing a job — taking a booking, sending the confirmation, remembering to follow up. I care about the boring parts: clean data, sane backend structure, nothing that falls over on a busy Saturday.

I usually work the whole flow solo: scope it out with whoever's asking, build the backend and the interface, wire up WhatsApp and email so nothing gets forgotten, and stay close after launch to fix what actually breaks — not just what looks broken. Studying cybersecurity alongside that pushes me to default to systems that are hardened from the start, instead of patched after something goes wrong.
